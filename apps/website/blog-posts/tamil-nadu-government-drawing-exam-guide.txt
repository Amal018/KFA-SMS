For students who are serious about art, the Tamil Nadu Government Technical Examination in Drawing and Painting is a recognised qualification. It is conducted by the State's Directorate of Government Examinations and is popular with students who want a formal credential alongside school.

## Who is it for?

The exam suits students who:

- Want a structured, certificate-backed path in drawing and painting
- Are considering careers in art teaching, design, illustration or architecture
- Enjoy disciplined practice and want clear goals

## What does the course cover?

Preparation usually includes the following areas. At Kalalaya, our one-year [professional course](course-details.php#professional) covers:

- Free-hand outline and model drawing
- Painting — water colour or oil painting
- Design — textile design or interior design
- Geometrical drawing
- Fashion sketching and illustration

## How long does preparation take?

Our professional course runs for one year, with classes on two days a week (Wednesday to Friday, any two days) for two hours a day. Regular home practice between classes makes a big difference.

## Important: check the official notification

Exam dates, grades, eligibility, fees and application procedures are announced by the Directorate of Government Examinations, Tamil Nadu, and can change from year to year. Always check the latest official notification before applying — we help our students keep track of the dates each year.

## Tips for students

1. **Practise daily**, even for 20 minutes — consistency beats long weekend sessions.
2. **Work from real objects** for model drawing, not only from photos.
3. **Time yourself** on practice papers so the exam day pace feels familiar.
4. **Keep your instruments ready** — sharpened pencils, a good eraser and geometry tools.
